Scope of the evidence

The main GPU study uses public Helios jobs, British OPSD electricity and solar records, and UK carbon intensity. Deadlines, equipment power, storage and site relocation remain explicit assumptions. GPU reservation does not prove TTFT/TPOT. A 15-minute model does not validate millisecond response. Aggregate GPUs do not certify CPU, RAM or network topology. Daily peak charges are a research proxy, not a monthly bill. Battery capital costs are excluded; ROI is not established.

See the report and docs for sources and formulas. The explorer shows the default online replay, with __DEFAULT_RESERVE__ extra reserved GPUs.
